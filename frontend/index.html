Process

How DocuTrade Works

From shipment creation to blockchain verification in four simple steps.

1

Create Shipment

Exporter creates a shipment with origin, destination, products, and trade details. The shipment enters the workflow.

2

Upload Documents

Upload invoices, certificates, and inspection reports. Each document is SHA-256 hashed and stored with version control.

3

Review & Approve

Inspectors review and approve documents. Approved document hashes are anchored on the Arbitrum blockchain network.

4

Verify & Trace

Anyone can verify document authenticity, trace the shipment, and check blockchain proof via QR code or public URL.
